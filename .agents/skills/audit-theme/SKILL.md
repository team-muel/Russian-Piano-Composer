---
name: audit-theme
description: Audit a generated theme for structural validity, style evidence, originality, and developmental potential.
---

Audit checklist:
1. **Structural Validity** — Is the theme well-formed? Correct pitch ranges, valid rhythms, proper measure structure.
2. **Russian-Style Evidence** — Does the theme exhibit features classified as OBSERVED or LITERATURE for Russian style? List supporting evidence.
3. **Developability** — Assess: fragmentability, inversion viability, augmentation/diminution potential, sequence potential, rhythmic identity, contour identity, harmonic reinterpretability.
4. **Novelty** — How distant is the theme from known corpus themes? Use interval n-grams, contour, rhythm similarity.
5. **Nearest Corpus Similarity** — Identify the closest theme(s) in the corpus. Report distance metric and identity.
6. **Copy Risk** — Does the theme risk being perceived as a copy after transposition or rhythmic modification? Separate from novelty assessment.
7. **Suspicious Metric Behavior** — Do any critic scores seem artificially inflated or gamed? Flag anomalies.
