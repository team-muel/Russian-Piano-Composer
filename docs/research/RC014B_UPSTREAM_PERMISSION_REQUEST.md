Subject: Permission Inquiry / Reuse Terms for Tonal Piano Corpus (Computational Musicology Research)

Dear Hector Bellmann,

We are researchers working on an open-source computational musicology project (Russian Piano Composer) investigating late-Romantic and early-20th-century piano style and structural representation.

We have reviewed your impressive and beautifully engraved dataset in the Tonal-Piano-Corpus repository (https://github.com/hectorbellmann-art/Tonal-Piano-Corpus), particularly your source-linked MusicXML transcriptions and high-resolution TIFF scans for Anton Rubinstein and Sergei Prokofiev.

We would like to formally request your clarification and permission regarding the following research use-cases:

1. Computational Feature Extraction:
   Parsing and analyzing your MusicXML files to compute abstract structural and statistical descriptors (e.g. pitch-class distributions, formal segmentation, voice-leading metrics).

2. Non-Vendored Reference Access:
   Referencing your repository at a pinned git commit hash (e.g., 3f5a08e9b2360c11aea5d6d384eb84e7845b793c) in our open-source scripts so researchers can automatically download and verify the scores locally on their own machines.

3. Potential Secondary Archiving / Redistribution:
   Whether you would permit redistributing selected MusicXML score encodings (with full citation, credit to you as engraver, and links to your original repository) within an academic dataset repository, or alternatively, whether you would consider adding a standard open license (such as Creative Commons CC-BY 4.0 or CC0) to your public repository.

We have noted your careful separation of copyright-restricted works in your README and have strictly confined our research to works whose underlying compositions are in the public domain.

Thank you very much for creating and sharing this wonderful musicological resource with the community. We look forward to your guidance on how you would prefer your corpus to be licensed and cited.

Sincerely,

The Russian Piano Composer Project Team
https://github.com/team-muel/Russian-Piano-Composer
